# Village & Pillage Backport

All of 1.14 Village & Pillage, on Minecraft 1.12.2. It needs Backport Core on the client and the server.

> 💬 [Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/kxQvMDJBTN) for bug reports and questions.

![A village at night, lit by a lantern, with a ravager and pillager behind the villagers](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2169/791/vp-village-night-lantern-raid-jpg.jpg)

## Villages

Villages are now 1.14's villages: plains, desert, savanna, taiga, and snowy tundra. You will find farms, animal pens, lamp posts, a meeting point with its bell, an iron golem, unemployed villagers waiting for work and, now and then, an abandoned zombie village.

Workstations spawn naturally inside village structures. Villages are built from modern blocks added into this mod. Cats can be found in villages.

Mods that add village buildings, like Waystones and RLCraft, add them to 1.14 villages too.

## Villagers

Villagers take a job from a nearby workstation and go to work every day. They sell 1.14's trades, level up from Novice to Master, and restock twice a day at their workstation.

Prices climb on trades you use a lot and drift back down when you leave them alone. Villagers remember how you treat them and gossip about it. Curing a zombie villager gives the biggest discount, and the cured villager keeps its old trades.

![A trade that has gone up in price](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/1900/417/demandpricing-png.png)

At dusk they head home to their beds. They breed when they have food and a free bed, so modern breeders work. A scared villager runs and calls an iron golem.

![The 1.14 trade screen](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/1900/425/villagergui-png.png)

![Villagers asleep in their beds](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2173/981/vp-villagers-asleep-jpg.jpg)

Every workstation does its 1.14 job. The fletching table cuts wood the way the stonecutter cuts stone, and the smithing table upgrades diamond gear to netherite when a mod adds netherite.

![The fletching table cutting planks](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/1900/418/fletching-png.png)

Breaking and replacing a librarian's lectern rerolls its books, and each roll shows up in chat. Hover over the message to turn it off.

![A librarian's roll message with its tooltip](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2173/994/vp-librarian-roll-tooltip-jpg.jpg)

## Raids

Carry Bad Omen into a village and a raid starts. Waves of pillagers, vindicators, evokers, witches and ravagers come at the village while the villagers hide in their houses. Win and you are the Hero of the Village, with cheaper trades and gifts from the villagers.

Pillager patrols roam the world from the fifth day, and pillager outposts generate with their watchtowers and caged golems. The crossbow is in too, with its enchantments.

![A raid in progress](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2173/989/vp-raid-in-progress-jpg.jpg)

![A pillager outpost](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2169/815/vp-pillager-outpost-jpg.jpg)

## Everything else

The wandering trader shows up now and then with two llamas and leaves after 40 minutes.

![The wandering trader crossing a bridge](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2169/789/vp-wandering-trader-cat-dock-jpg.jpg)

Cats come in eleven coats. Tame one with raw fish and it will bring you gifts in the morning.

Foxes live in taigas. They sleep through the day and hunt at night, carrying whatever they find in their mouths. Sweet berry bushes grow there too, so watch out for the thorns.

Bamboo jungles are home to pandas, each with its own personality. Bamboo makes scaffolding you can climb.

![Pandas in a bamboo jungle](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2169/816/vp-bamboo-jungle-pandas-jpg.jpg)

The campfire cooks food without fuel. Put a hay bale under it for a signal fire.

You also get the new flowers and dyes, suspicious stew, brown mooshrooms, leather horse armor, the Globe banner pattern, every new slab, stair and wall, smooth stone, signs in every wood, stripped logs, and blue ice.

Vanilla's textures switch to the 1.14 look, and you can turn that off.

Every feature can be turned off in Backport Core's settings screen, under Mod Options. Each setting has a plain name and a one-line description, and search finds any of them.

![Village & Pillage's settings](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2174/555/vp-settings-new-png.png)

## Compatibility

**[Update Aquatic Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/update-aquatic-backport)**: the wandering trader sells sea pickles, and desert houses get sea pickles. Its kelp, seagrass and sea pickles go in the composter, the stonecutter cuts prismarine, cats scare phantoms away, and stray cats and foxes hunt baby turtles.

**[Caves & Cliffs Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/caves-cliffs-backport)**: in its 1.18 worlds, villages and outposts generate on 1.18 terrain and the ground is shaped around them, and its terrain grows this mod's bamboo, berry bushes and flowers. The composter takes its lush cave plants, foxes walk on powder snow, and the wandering trader sells pointed dripstone.

**[Buzzy Bees Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/buzzy-bees-backport)**: campfires calm bees, bees pollinate cornflowers, lilies of the valley and wither roses and grow berry bushes, bells ring from redstone, and village golems crack.

**[Nether Update Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/nether-backport)**: netherite upgrades use the smithing table, piglins carry crossbows and love bells, the composter takes nether plants, the soul campfire cooks what the campfire cooks, and wither roses grow on soul soil.

**[Wild Update Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/wild-update-backport)**: pillager outposts get the allay cage and goat horns, the wandering trader sells propagules, the composter takes mangrove plants, and scaffolding burns as fuel.

**[Trails & Tales Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/trails-tales-backport)**: camels spawn in desert villages, the smithing table takes its templates, and its bamboo is this mod's bamboo.

**[Tricky Trials Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/trials-backport)**: pillager captains drop its ominous bottle, which gives its Bad Omen: raids in villages, ominous trials at trial spawners. The loom takes its banner patterns, the stonecutter cuts tuff, and the composter takes its plants.

**[Game Drops Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/game-drops-backport)**: villagers and the wandering trader trade as in 26.3, with its items. The wandering trader sells golden dandelions, name tags, poplar saplings, logs and shelf mushrooms. The composter takes its plants and the stonecutter cuts its concrete stairs and slabs. Cartography tables copy explorer maps, and village explorer maps lead to this mod's villages.

**Dynamic Trees**: bamboo jungles grow its jungle trees, and village trees are its own where it grows the biome's trees.

**Other mods**: works with Open Terrain Generator (RLCraft Dregora), Biomes O' Plenty, Realistic Terrain Generation, Antique Atlas, JEI Villagers, TradeStages Legacy, Scape and Run: Parasites, and Hunter Illager.

Using Village Names? Set `Activate New Village Generator` to `false` in `config/VillageNames4/newvillages/village_gen.cfg` to get 1.14 villages.

## Commands

`/locate structure #minecraft:village` finds the nearest village, or name one type like `minecraft:village_desert`.

`/village` shows the beds around you and who has claimed them.

`/rollmessages off` stops the librarian roll messages for you, and `on` brings them back.

`/raid start` starts a raid on the village you're in, and `/wanderingtrader spawn` brings the trader to you. Both are for operators.

## Requirements

Minecraft 1.12.2 with Forge 14.23.5.2847 or newer, and [Backport Core](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/backport-core) 1.4.7 or newer. The CurseForge app installs Backport Core and Fluidlogged API with it.

Delete the old version of this mod before updating.
